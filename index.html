About Us

KalapaTEE was created from the playful concept of a kalapati (pigeon) and the warmth and familiarity of a Filipino home. Inspired by everyday Filipino experiences, culture, and the realities faced by ordinary Filipinos, the brand uses creative shirt designs to express identity, freedom, hope, and social awareness.
