BRAND
STORY

KalapaTEE was created from the playful concept of a kalapat (pigeon) and the warmth and familiarity of a Filipino home. Inspired by everyday Filipino experiences, culture, and traditions, the brand uses creative shirt designs to express identity, freedom, hope, and social awareness.

Combining the familiar image of a kalapat with the comforting idea of tilaga, KalapaTEE aims to create clothing that connects Filipino culture with meaningful messages and relatable experiences. Through unique, comfortable, and accessible designs, the brand brings creativity and cultural expression closer to its customers.

Our tagline "From the Sky, to Your Bahay," KalapaTEE represents the idea of bringing something meaningful and memorable from the skies into every Filipino home.

MISSION

KalapaTEE's mission is to create unique, comfortable, and accessible shirts inspired by Filipino culture, everyday experiences, and social realities. We aim to provide creative designs that allow people to express themselves, celebrate their identity, and share meaningful messages while maintaining a friendly, inclusive, and customer-centered shopping experience.

VISION

KalapaTEE envisions becoming a recognizable Filipino-inspired clothing brand that connects creativity, cultural identity, and social awareness through meaningful apparel. We aspire to expand our collections, reach more customers, and build a community that values self-expression, freedom, hope, and the everyday experiences of the Filipino people.

ABOUT THE TEAM

KalapaTEE is a project developed by TEAM NILAGA, a group of students who share an interest in creativity, Filipino culture, and meaningful visual expression. Through this project, the team combines ideas in branding, design, and web development to create an accessible online platform for showcasing Filipino-inspired shirt designs. By incorporating creativity, culture, and everyday experiences, the team aims to present a brand that is playful, relatable, and connected to the experiences of ordinary Filipinos. By working together in planning, designing, developing, and evaluating the website, the team hopes to establish KalapaTEE as a creative expression of Filipino identity and everyday life.
